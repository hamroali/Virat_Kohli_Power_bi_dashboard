🏏 Virat Kohli Career Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that explores Virat Kohli's international cricket career: runs, centuries, averages, performance against each opponent, and how his form changed year by year.

![Dashboard preview](dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Project Goal

Turn raw career statistics into a single-page, interactive report that answers questions like:
- How many runs, centuries and fifties has he scored?
- Which opponents did he score the most runs against?
- Which years were his strongest, and when did his form drop?

## 📊 Dashboard Features

| Visual | What it shows |
|---|---|
| KPI cards | 100s, 50s, 30+ scores, average runs, total runs, highest score |
| Bar chart | Runs by opponent |
| Donut chart | Number of matches by opponent team |
| Area/line chart | Runs by year |
| Year slicer | Filters every visual to the selected period |
| Career header | Debut years in ODI, T20 and Test formats |

All visuals react to the year slicer, so any period of his career can be explored in seconds.

## 💡 Key Insights (2014–2022 view)

- **16K runs** with an average of **48.13**, **55 centuries**, **89 fifties** and a highest score of **254**
- **Australia** is the opponent he scored the most runs against (**3.2K**), followed by England (2.9K) and West Indies (2.5K)
- His **peak year was 2017** with **2.8K runs**; 2016–2019 was his strongest stretch
- From **2020** his yearly runs dropped clearly (0.8K in 2020, 0.5K in 2022)
